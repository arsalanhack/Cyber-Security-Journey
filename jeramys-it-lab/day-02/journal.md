**Topic: Interfaces and cables**

**2026-07-30**

On this day I learned the basics of Interfaces and Cables.

In my words:
- A Registered Jack(RJ) is the head at the end of a copper cable, it is an industry standard
- An Ethernet is a collection of network protocols. It defines how data is wired, formatted and transmitted across a LAN.
- Internet speed is measured in bits per second. one kilo bit(1000 bits) all the way to 1 tera bit(1 trillion bits).

I understood these concepts very well, it made me realise the industry standards how everything is so standardised.

The definition of ethernet confused me a bit, I always thought ethernet was just the ethernet cable. Now I know it is more than just that.


**2026-07-31**

On this day I learned Ethernet standards(IEEE), copper cables and pairs of wires used to transmit and receive data.

In my own words:
- Ethernet standards defined by IEEE, these are some standards that the whole world follows when manufacturing these cables.
    e.g: Speed: 10mbps - Common name: Ethernet - IEEE standard name: 802.3i - Informal name: 10Base-T - Length: 100 meters, etc.
- Copper cables are also called UTP copper cables. UTP stands for Unshielded, twisted, pair. They have 4 pairs of twisted wires, 8 wires in total.
- 10Base-T(Ethernet) and 100Base-T(fast ethernet) use 2 pair of cables, 4 wires in total.
- 1000Base-T(Gigabit ethernet) and 10GBase-T(10Gig ethernet) use all 4 pairs of cables, 8 wires in total.
