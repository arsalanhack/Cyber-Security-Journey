**Topic: Fundamentals of Networks**

I learned what a Network, Client, Servers, Switches, Routers, Firewalls are.

In my own words:
- A network is a collection of end-hosts(computer, server) connected with each other to share resources.
- A client is a device that accesses the services made available by a server.
- A server is a device that provides services to clients
- A switch is a device that lets a LAN(local area network) to connect. it has many interfaces to let many end-hosts connect.
- A router is a device that lets the LAN to connect with other LANs across the internet. They are used to send data over the internet.
- A firewall monitors and controls network traffic based on rules. There are two types of firewalls:
    Network firewall: which is a hardware device that filters traffic between networks.
    Host-based firewall: which is a software application that acts as another layer of protection just in case.
